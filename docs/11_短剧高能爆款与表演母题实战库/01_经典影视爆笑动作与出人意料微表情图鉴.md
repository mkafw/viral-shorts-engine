## 🎭 一、四大经典影视微表情图腾（中英 Prompt 对照）

### 1. 周星驰式冷面悲喜反差（Stephen Chow Archetype）
- **镜头表现**：上一秒深情庄重，下一秒嘴角抽搐；刀架在脖子上，手在发抖但强行优雅地理领带；极度谄媚瞬间切入毫无波动的死人脸。
- **英文 Prompt 指令**：
  ```text
  Acting: Stephen Chow comedic-irony style — sudden deadpan shift from melodramatic seriousness to a frozen awkward smile. Nervous eye-darting while body remains deceptively rigid. Micro-tremor in cheek, jaw clenching.
  ```

### 2. 小丑式病态狂躁与痛苦共存（Joker Unhinged Archetype）
- **镜头表现**：大笑时胸膛剧烈抽搐但眼角流出生理性痛苦眼泪；慢速失魂落魄的芭蕾舞步突然转为极速暴力爆发；低头从眉毛下方死死盯住镜头（库布里克凝视）。
- **英文 Prompt 指令**：
  ```text
  Acting: Joaquin Phoenix Joker style — painful hysterical laughter breaking into violent respiratory spasms, eyes wet with genuine misery while jaw stretches wide. Abrupt snap into dead cold Kubrick stare.
  ```

### 3. 《非人哉》神仙反差冷幽默（Deadpan Mythic Contrast）
- **镜头表现**：身负通天法力，面无表情做着现代打工人极度摆烂的事；全脸毫无波动的死鱼眼，面对大灾难默默喝一口奶茶。
- **英文 Prompt 指令**：
  ```text
  Acting: Modern mythic deadpan irony — absolute stoic emotionless face while holding divine celestial artifacts. Slow 90-degree head turn to glance dead-eyed at camera, sipping boba tea.
  ```

### 4. 成龙式打斗滑稽借力（Slapstick Action Physics）
- **镜头表现**：慌乱中顺手抄起折叠椅/梯子/雨伞迎击，打完之后甩着通红的手疯狂哈气喊痛。
- **英文 Prompt 指令**：
  ```text
  Acting: Jackie Chan kinetic physical comedy — panicked improvisation grabbing folding chair as shield, followed by comically shaking bruised hand and blowing on painful knuckles.
  ```

## 🛋️ 二、出人意料的奇葩坐姿与体态（静态画面自带笑点）

1. **西装大爷蹲（Bespoke Suit Crouching）**：
   - 身穿几十万顶级手工西装的霸总，开着跨国会议，整个人踩在真皮老板椅上抱膝深蹲吃泡面。
   - `Billionaire in tailored Italian bespoke suit fully crouching with both feet flat on the leather office chair, hugging knees like a street thug, casual deadpan expression.`
2. **悬空强撑坐（Hovering Phantom Squat）**：
   - 椅子被拉走坐空了，但为了面子死撑不倒，以 90 度空气马步悬空坐着，悠闲端咖啡。
   - `Comedic physical absurdity: man hovering in mid-air in a 90-degree invisible chair squat, sipping espresso with total unbothered dignity.`
3. **倒挂无赖瘫（Upside-down Meeting Slouch）**：
   - 脑袋朝下悬空垂在地毯上，双脚高高搭在投影仪上划手机。
   - `Upside-down slouch posture, head dangling toward floor from chair, feet resting high on conference table, casually scrolling phone.`

## 🏃 三、出人意料的肢体动作与社死挽尊

1. **刚挨揍先正领带（Dignity Restoration）**：
   - 被人一拳轰上墙壁摔下来，慢条斯理拍灰，翘兰花指扶正领带。
   - `Comedic recovery: after being brutally shoved against wall, calmly brushes lint off shoulders, meticulously adjusts tie with delicate pinky finger extended.`
2. **看！飞碟！（Theatrical Misdirection）**：
   - 生死关头突然浮夸指天惊呼，趁对手抬头，光速贴地四肢并用溜走。
   - `Suddenly points aggressively toward the sky with gasping theatrical awe; the moment enemy turns, scuttles away on all fours at comical speed.`
3. **平地摔变单手俯卧撑（Faceplant to Pushup）**：
   - 霸气出场突然脚滑摔倒，在脸部触地前 0.1 秒转为单手俯卧撑并抛媚眼。
   - `Tripping awkwardly on red carpet but instantly transitioning the hard face-plant into aggressive one-arm pushups, winking at camera.`
